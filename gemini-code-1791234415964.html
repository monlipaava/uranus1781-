A New World in the Night Sky

In 1781, a music teacher looked into his homemade telescope and found a new giant planet. It was the first planet found in modern history.

Cool Facts

🌀 It rolls like a ball

Most planets spin like a top. Uranus spins on its side! Because of this, night and day at its poles can last 42 years.

❄️ Extremely cold

Uranus is an Ice Giant. Its atmosphere is made of hydrogen, helium, and methane ice. Temperatures drop to -224 °C.

🔭 A lucky accident

Herschel first thought he found a comet. Later, scientists calculated its path and proved it was a massive planet far behind Saturn.

How it Happened

1. The Music Teacher

William Herschel was a musician who loved the night sky. Because good telescopes were expensive, he learned how to melt metal and polish mirrors by hand.

2. The Discovery Night

In his small garden in England, he noticed a faint blue object. It moved across the sky night after night, unlike the fixed stars.

3. Doubling the System

This discovery doubled the known size of our Solar System and made Herschel famous all over the world.
